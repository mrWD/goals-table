GoalsTable — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

GoalsTable is a personal tracker for yearly goals. The shortest honest summary: your goals never leave your device, and we could not read them if we wanted to. Once a day the app sends anonymous usage counts, described below, which you can switch off.

What we store

None of your goals. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that holds user data. Your goals live in your browser’s storage (on the web) or in the app’s own private storage (in the installed app). Deleting your browsing data or the app deletes them — which is why the app keeps offering you an export. All we keep are the anonymous totals described under “Usage statistics”.

What leaves your device

Your goals never do. GoalsTable queries no catalogue: everything it shows, it holds locally, and it works with the network switched off. The one request it makes is the daily usage report below; with that switched off, it makes none at all.

Usage statistics

The app counts, on your device, which screens you open and which features you use (adding, completing and moving a goal, the daily blocks, adding a resource, exporting and importing a backup), and how many errors of each kind occurred — by type, such as “TypeError”, never the error message. Once a day, at the first launch of the day, it sends those counts to our server together with the app version, the platform (iOS, Android or web), the interface language, the date, the week the app was first used, and whether this is the first report ever, this week or this month.

It sends no identifier of any kind — no device, user or advertising ID — and none of your goals, titles, notes or anything else you type. Your IP address is used only to deliver the request and to limit how often one address can send: it is held as a salted hash in memory for ten minutes and is never stored or logged by our code. The server adds each report into totals per app and per day, week and month; nothing is kept per device, and the totals are deleted after 400 days. The server runs on Vercel, which keeps its own short-lived request logs, and the totals are stored with Upstash.

To turn it off: Profile → Share anonymous usage statistics → Off. Nothing more is sent, and the counts not yet sent are deleted. On the web, if your browser sends Global Privacy Control, it starts switched off. Nothing here is used for advertising or to track you across apps or websites.

Backups

An exported backup — JSON or CSV — is an ordinary file created at your request. It goes wherever you send it — your Files, your drive, your email — and never to us.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection, under Apple’s terms; it reaches us only as anonymous crash reports, and never includes anything you typed into the app.
