GoalsTable — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

GoalsTable is a personal tracker for yearly goals. The shortest honest summary: your goals never leave your device, and we could not read them if we wanted to.

What we store

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that holds user data. Your goals live in your browser’s storage (on the web) or in the app’s own private storage (in the installed app). Deleting your browsing data or the app deletes them — which is why the app keeps offering you an export.

What leaves your device

Nothing. GoalsTable has no backend of its own and queries no catalogue: everything it shows, it holds locally. The app works with the network switched off, and behaves the same way when it is on.

Analytics

None, on the web and in the installed app alike. No page views, no events, no identifiers, no crash reporting.

Backups

An exported backup — JSON or CSV — is an ordinary file created at your request. It goes wherever you send it — your Files, your drive, your email — and never to us.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection, under Apple’s terms; it reaches us only as anonymous crash reports, and never includes anything you typed into the app.
